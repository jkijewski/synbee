# app-api Specification

## Purpose

Defines the API the mobile app uses: a separate listener with the hub's own TLS key, reachable from the home network and, when the owner allows it, from outside, with pairing and the requests of a paired phone.

How this is built and why: [0021](../../../docs/decisions/0021-app-api.md). The routes, requests and answers in detail: [openapi/app-api.json](../../../openapi/app-api.json), an OpenAPI 3.1 document generated from `hub/src/app-api-schema.ts`.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Separate TLS listener, home network unless allowed
The app API SHALL be served on its own port (default 8443), separate from the web interface, over TLS 1.2 or later with a key generated by the hub on first start. It SHALL refuse connections from addresses outside private ranges unless the owner switched on "Using the app away from home" and entered the outside address (a host name or IP) and port, and allowed that phone away from home on the App users page (off for a phone paired at home, on for one paired away). Ping and pairing SHALL be accepted from outside always: pairing needs a one-time code the owner made. A phone not allowed away from home SHALL NOT be told the outside address. It SHALL serve only the app API routes; every other path SHALL answer 404. After 20 refused tokens in 10 minutes, an address SHALL be refused for a while.

#### Scenario: Request from outside, not allowed
- **WHEN** a request to the app port comes from a public address while away-from-home use is off
- **THEN** it is refused with 403

#### Scenario: Away from home
- **GIVEN** away-from-home use is on at `myhome.synology.me:18443`, forwarded by the router to the NAS's app port
- **WHEN** a paired phone on mobile data opens the app
- **THEN** it reaches the hub at that address, checks the hub's pinned key, and shows its rooms and actions

#### Scenario: Pairing from outside
- **WHEN** a pairing request with a valid code comes from outside the home network, even with away-from-home use off
- **THEN** the phone is paired, and other requests from outside are served only once away-from-home use is on

#### Scenario: Web interface route on the app port
- **WHEN** a request for `/api/devices` arrives on the app port
- **THEN** the answer is 404

### Requirement: Where to connect
`GET /api/app/v1/connection` SHALL give a paired phone the hub's home addresses and, when away-from-home use is on, its outside address and port. The pairing QR code SHALL carry the outside address too. The app SHALL try the home address first on Wi-Fi and the outside address first elsewhere, and SHALL let the user enter the outside address and port by hand.

### Requirement: Pinned hub key
The pairing QR code SHALL carry the SHA-256 fingerprint of the hub's public key. The app SHALL trust exactly that key, instead of certificate authorities and host names, and SHALL send nothing, the pairing code included, to a server with another key.

#### Scenario: Another server at the hub's address
- **WHEN** the app connects to an address whose server presents another key
- **THEN** the app stops before sending anything and says the hub's identity doesn't match

### Requirement: Versioned contract
Routes SHALL live under `/api/app/v1/`. A change that breaks a published route SHALL use a new version prefix, and the hub SHALL keep serving the previous version for at least one release. Request and answer bodies SHALL be JSON, and answers SHALL not be cached. Every route SHALL be described in the OpenAPI document; the hub SHALL serialize answers and check requests by the same schemas the document is generated from, refuse at start a route that isn't in them, and its tests SHALL fail when the committed document differs from the generated one. Errors SHALL be `{"error": "…"}` with a sentence to show the user.

#### Scenario: Hub check
- **WHEN** the app requests `GET /api/app/v1/ping` without a token
- **THEN** the hub answers with `{"hub":"synbee","api":1,"version":…}`, and nothing about its members or devices

### Requirement: Pairing request
`POST /api/app/v1/pair` with a pairing code and a phone name SHALL pair the phone to the code's member and return a phone token once. The hub SHALL store only a hash of the token. Wrong codes SHALL be limited to 10 attempts a minute per address.

#### Scenario: Successful pairing
- **WHEN** the app sends a valid code and the name "Pixel 8"
- **THEN** it receives a token and the member's name, and "Pixel 8" appears under that member in the web interface

### Requirement: Signed-in requests
Every other route SHALL need `Authorization: Bearer <token>` of a paired, unrevoked phone, checked on the matched route, and SHALL record when the phone was last used. A missing, unknown or revoked token SHALL be answered with 401.

#### Scenario: Test request
- **WHEN** a paired phone requests `GET /api/app/v1/me`
- **THEN** the hub answers with the member's name, the phone's name, the hub's version and how many actions and rooms the member may use

### Requirement: Actions
`GET /api/app/v1/actions` SHALL list the actions the phone's member may run (not drafts, not revoked), in the owner's order, with their id, name, icon and description. `POST /api/app/v1/actions/{id}/run` SHALL run one of them and answer whether it succeeded and, if not, which step failed and why. Drafts, revoked and missing actions SHALL all answer 404. Running an action from the app SHALL be an action event for automations, like a run from the web interface.

#### Scenario: Running an action
- **WHEN** a paired phone runs "Evening"
- **THEN** its steps run and the answer is `{"ok":true}`

#### Scenario: Revoked action
- **WHEN** a phone tries to run an action revoked for its member
- **THEN** the answer is 404, as for a missing action

### Requirement: Home screen by room
`GET /api/app/v1/home` SHALL return the member's allowed rooms that have actions, in the owner's order, each with its name, icon and colour (the same colour as in the web interface), and the member's actions with their icon and the allowed rooms they belong to. An action belongs to the rooms of the devices it uses; one that uses no room's devices SHALL have no rooms (the app shows it under "Other"); one whose rooms are all revoked for the member SHALL NOT be listed.

#### Scenario: A revoked room
- **GIVEN** "Wieczór" uses a light in "Salon", and "Wszystko off" uses lights in "Salon" and "Garaż"
- **WHEN** "Garaż" is revoked for Kasia
- **THEN** her home lists only "Salon", with both actions under it

### Requirement: Action state on the tile
Each action on the home screen SHALL carry its state: `active` when every device it sets shows what the action leaves it showing (its steps merged in order) (on/off exactly, brightness within 5 % of its range, colour within 10° of hue, white temperature within 10 mired, other values exactly; "off" matches an off device whatever else the step says), `partly` when only some do, `inactive` when none does, or null when no state is known. It SHALL also carry the tile's colour state (`power`: `on`, `partly`, `off` or null), by the kind of action: a scene (setting colour, white temperature or brightness) is `on` only while every device shows what the scene leaves it showing (its steps merged in order), and `off` otherwise, so of several scenes on the same lights only the showing one is coloured; a plain "on" action is `on` while what it switches is on; an "off" action is always `off`. The app colours the tile by it and marks an active action with a check. It SHALL also carry a status line: what the owner chose for the tile, or by default the words of what it switches ("Off", "2 of 3 on", "On · red · 30 %"). The app SHALL re-read the home screen every 5 seconds while it is open and in front, and right after running an action.

#### Scenario: Two scenes on the same strips
- **GIVEN** "Bedroom red" and "Bedroom lights on" set the same three strips to red and to warm white
- **WHEN** the strips show red
- **THEN** only "Bedroom red" is coloured, and "Bedroom lights off" stays grey

#### Scenario: Lights partly on
- **GIVEN** "Bedroom off" switches off a group of two lights
- **WHEN** one of them is on
- **THEN** the tile is "partly" with the line "1 of 2 on"

### Requirement: Action details
`GET /api/app/v1/actions/{id}/details` SHALL list what an allowed action touches: each device, and each light group with its devices, with the device's name, room, state in words, readings with alarms marked, battery, online state and when it was last heard from. Devices in rooms revoked for the member SHALL be included, since the member may run the action.

#### Scenario: Long press on a tile
- **WHEN** a member long-presses "Bedroom lights off"
- **THEN** the app shows the group "Bedroom" and its three lights, each with its state

### Requirement: Room page
`GET /api/app/v1/rooms/{id}` SHALL list the devices of an allowed room (main room and "also shown here"), lights first, then switches, sensors and others, each in words as in the action details. A revoked or missing room SHALL answer 404. The page SHALL only read.

#### Scenario: Long press on a room tab
- **WHEN** a member long-presses the "Bedroom" tab
- **THEN** the app shows the room's lights, switches and sensors with their states

### Requirement: Read-only except actions
Running an action SHALL be the only change the app API makes. It SHALL NOT switch single devices or change any configuration. Devices, rooms and actions a member may not see SHALL answer as if they didn't exist.
